Accessible profile is the base layer, not a separate mode: solid surfaces, max contrast, a legibility-tuned font (Atkinson Hyperlegible), larger hit targets, and thicker focus rings — the expressive themes are deltas layered on top. A− / A+ rescales every label live (works in any mode). The legibility rule: text always sits on a near-solid substrate — the glass lives in the frame, edges, and shadow, never behind the words — so contrast stays WCAG AA over any map.
